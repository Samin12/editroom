# cutroom-cut evaluation (Samin's YouTube course)

Generated by `cargo run --release -p cutroom-cut --example eval_borumi -- --write`.

Ground truth: the current Borumi timeline of the course project (cut by a multi-agent LLM workflow plus manual edits). A raw word counts as *cut* when its narration recording is on the timeline but the word is not inside a kept mic segment. Some of those cuts were editorial (content dropped for the narrative), so recall cannot reach 100%.

## How detect works

- **Restarts / retakes**: for every word, earlier positions within 120 words that share its bigram (or a rare word) are restart candidates. A pair is shifted back while the word before the attempt equals its last word (the same edit, aligned on the true restart, keeping the later copy). Score = weighted share of the abandoned attempt that is said again (stemmed, stop words weigh 0.25) + whether both attempts start a clause + exact-prefix length − sentences inside the attempt. The cut runs from the attempt's clause start to just before the restart, keeping the restart's lead-in ("And", "So"). Chains of attempts merge, so only the last one stays.
- **Stutters**: immediate repeats of 2-4 words (auto unless the first copy is a whole sentence); single function-word repeats go to review; emphasis ("very, very"), sentence joins ("this. This is") and "bit by bit" are left alone.
- **Fillers**: um / uh / erm (not "like" / "so"); tokens under 60 ms are recognition artefacts and skipped.
- **Silences**: pauses over 300 ms inside a take keep 120 ms next to each word; dead air before a take's first and after its last word keeps 250 ms. Never inside a word.
- **Abandoned takes**: a take whose word trigrams are mostly re-said by one of the next three takes, unless the matching words sit at the same moments (multi-camera takes of one performance).

## Known gaps

- About 30% of the ground-truth cut words fall in no candidate at all: editorial cuts (a sentence dropped for the story) and abandoned clauses that are never re-said. No repetition signal exists for those; they need an LLM pass over the script, not this detector.
- Paraphrased restarts that share no bigram with the attempt are missed; restarts whose first copy the ground truth preferred count as false positives above (see swap-aware precision).
- Parakeet word timings run edge to edge, so many pauses hide inside word durations; silence detection only sees gaps between words.
- The scoring was hand-tuned on this one project (the split-half numbers agree); expect to recalibrate on other speakers.
